# Search Console baseline, 10 September 2026

Raw export covering the three months to 8 September 2026, web search, firstownersreference.com. Kept so the October comparison is a diff against recorded numbers rather than a recollection.

Forty-seven of the site's forty-eight titles exceeded display length throughout this period, and twenty-five descriptions ran two to four times over. All of it was corrected on 9 September, the day before this export, which makes this the last clean reading of the old listings.

The single number to compare: click-through rate across positions four to ten. It was 0.25 percent here, on 29,530 impressions and 74 clicks, against an ordinary expectation of two to six percent for that band.

Secondary comparisons worth making, in order:

Chapter one. It held 14,756 impressions at position 7.3 and produced six clicks, a rate of 0.04 percent. It is the largest page on the site by impressions and the clearest single test of whether the title work moved anything.

The 10 percent rule query family. Three variants of "superyacht annual operating cost 10 percent purchase price" held positions between 4.7 and 6.2 across 267 impressions with no clicks at all, while the AEO testing showed the chapter's framing being reproduced in generated answers. If those queries are still at zero in October, the citation is the outcome and the click is not available.

The calculator, which is the site's entire click engine: 14,212 impressions at position 8.3 for 67 of the site's 106 clicks. It was re-angled on 9 September to stop colliding with Foreland's version, so both its position and its share of total clicks are worth watching.

The two new reference pages, insurance cost and depreciation, which did not exist at the time of this export.

Also worth watching: the site draws long conversational questions about family offices and charter brokers, at positions between 34 and 81. There is no material on either subject. If those impressions grow, the demand is real.

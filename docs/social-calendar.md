# Social calendar: launch run to Monaco

Drafted 2026-07-31. The post-by-post schedule the `social-strategy.md` operations section calls for. Covers the eight weeks from Monday 2026-08-03 to the close of Monaco Yacht Show week (2026-09-23 to 09-26). Sits under `marketing-plan.md` and serves the `social-campaign-24m.md` targeting layer.

## How to read this

One block per week. Chapter campaign named at the top, per `social-strategy.md` and `marketing-plan.md`.

For each week the publication LinkedIn page (4 posts) and Instagram (3 posts) are specified in full: pillar, the studio template and theme to use, the hook, the source, and the single conversion point. Cards are built in the [social card studio](https://firstownersreference-social.vercel.app).

The two editors' personal accounts are given as angles, not scripted posts. Per strategy, Jack and Daniel write in their own register, are not approval-gated, and pick the angle that fits their voice. Jack posts Tue, Thu, Sat. Daniel posts Mon, Wed, Fri.

Formats: **LI square** is 1200 × 1200, **4:5** is 1080 × 1350. Every carousel closes on a source card.

Pillar mix across a week holds to roughly 2 cited claim, 1 position, 1 behind the page, 1 reader or interview, per strategy.

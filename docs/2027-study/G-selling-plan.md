# Selling the yacht: integration plan

Status: plan, 14 November 2026. Nothing drafted or edited yet.

## Why

The book follows the owner from deciding to buy through operating, and stops there. Every owner eventually sells. The selling side raises its own decisions, and several of them mirror material the book already covers from the buyer's side (commission, dual agency, days on market, the MOA). The structural read (F-structural-read.md, question 11) flagged the gap.

## Where it goes

**Home: chapter 06.** It already opens with "When to refit, when to sell" (paras 2 to 6) and the 30 percent rule of thumb. A new section, "Selling the yacht", follows para 6 and comes before "Refit cost benchmarks". Target 600 to 800 words in the essay.

**Chapter title.** Either keep "Refit" or retitle "Refit and resale" (sections.json title only; the URL slug 06-refit stays). Author's decision.

**Elsewhere, pointers only (one sentence each, no re-argument):**
- ch01 case: the owner who sold at 28 percent after 18 months; point to ch06.
- ch02: the days-on-market and price-cut figures, add one sentence on what they mean for a seller.
- ch03: commission and dual agency are explained from the buyer's side; one sentence noting the seller pays and signs the listing agreement, pointing to ch06.
- ch04: the MOA and closing section; one sentence that the same contract binds the seller, pointing to ch06.
- ch09: the hold period is a decision at purchase; add exit to the questions the team answers before buying.

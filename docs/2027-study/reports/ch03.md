# Chapter 03, How the industry actually works: editing report

Edits file: docs/2027-study/edits/ch03.json (32 edits: 15 fact, 11 line, 3 structure, 3 repetition). Every `old` string occurs once in its file, both paragraph operations match one paragraph, and a dry run of the full set parsed cleanly. The essay grows from 2,328 to about 2,485 words because the role definition and disclosure standard now live here.

## Overall assessment

The chapter is the evidence base for the book's central claim, and its commission, dual-agency, ownership and retrocession material is specific and sourced. The weaknesses were a wrong headline figure, a standfirst that contradicted the body, a shifting register, generalisations wider than the evidence, and no definition of the adviser the reader is told to hire first. All are addressed. The criticism is kept; sentences that asserted more than the chapter shows now say what it shows, and the disclosure standard gives the critique a rule to measure against.

## Factual concerns and status

**C4, commission arithmetic. Resolved.** Working:
- IYBA flat 10 percent on EUR 28 million: EUR 2.8 million.
- MYBA scale with the bands applied in euros: 10% x 10m = 1.0m; 5% x 10m = 0.5m; 2.5% x 8m = 0.2m. Total EUR 1.7 million. The essay's EUR 1.4 million matches neither norm (it is 5 percent of EUR 28 million).
- MYBA on USD 50 million: 1.0 + 0.5 + 0.75 = USD 2.25 million. Correct.
- Buyer-broker share: 4 to 5 percent of gross is 40 to 50 percent of the IYBA pool, but on EUR 28 million it would be EUR 1.12 to 1.4 million out of a MYBA pool of EUR 1.7 million (66 to 82 percent), and on USD 50 million the whole MYBA pool is only 4.5 percent. "The same arithmetic from either base" was therefore wrong. Para 4 now says the share is typically half the pool or a little less: 4 to 5 percent on the US base, about EUR 850,000 (3 percent) on EUR 28 million under MYBA.
- Source: the MYBA bands and a 50 percent buyer-broker share are both stated in the US Marshals shared-commission policy for the Amadea sale (https://amadea.s3.amazonaws.com/Auction_Documents/Shared%20Commission%20Memorandum.pdf), which applies the bands in US dollars. Applying them in euros, as the essay and data spread do, is an assumption.
- The ch03 data spread had the MYBA figure as EUR 1,950,000 (2.5 percent applied to EUR 18 million instead of 8 million) and the 1.4 to 2.8 range in its standfirst. Both corrected in data-spreads.json, and the buyer-broker row restated per base.

## Structural changes

- **Disclosure standard (author decision 1).** Para 25 is now the home of the rule: a party paid on completion, or by commission, can be engaged if the payment, its amount or rate, and who makes it are disclosed to the buyer in writing in advance and the party's role is named. Para 20 applies it to retrocessions; para 37 frames element 1 as "contingent earnings, and whether they are disclosed in writing". The FAQ "Who pays the yacht broker commission?" was corrected (not every introduced party is paid on closing) and now carries the same rule.

Adapted from ch05 39, which can now apply the definition to new build rather than introduce it.

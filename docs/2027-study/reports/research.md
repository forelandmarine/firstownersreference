## 1. US tariffs, EU ceiling (ch02)

Finding: the USTR notice of action of 23 July 2026 (Dockets USTR-2026-0265 and -0266), effective 24 July 2026, sets the EU's combined duty at 10 percent. Where a product's MFN duty is below 10 percent, the Section 301 duty tops it up to 10 percent. Where the MFN duty is 10 percent or more, no Section 301 duty applies. The 12.5 percent net-of-MFN rule covers Japan, Korea and Switzerland. The UK pays a flat 10 percent.
Source: https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Press/Releases/2026/FLIP%20301%20Investigation%20Final%20Action%20FRN%207-23-26%20FINAL.pdf
Decision: corrected. The essay now states the 10 percent combined figure, the zero-where-MFN-is-at-least-10 rule and the UK rate.

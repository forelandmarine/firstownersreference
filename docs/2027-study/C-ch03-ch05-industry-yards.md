## 1. Summary (top 10)

1. **MarineMax is being sold (ch03).** On 10 August 2026 MarineMax signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by Safe Harbor Marinas, a Blackstone Infrastructure portfolio company. The deal is USD 1.5 billion in cash at USD 53 per share, with closing expected by the end of 2026. Fraser and Northrop & Johnson will then sit inside a privately held, Blackstone-backed marina and superyacht-services group alongside IGY Marinas, not "inside MarineMax (NYSE: HZO)". The ch03 essay paragraph 15 and data spread rows for Fraser and N&J will be wrong for a 2027 reader. The deal strengthens the chapter's point about ownership concentration: brokerage owned alongside marinas and service yards.
2. **Denison is changing hands again (ch03).** On 7 to 9 September 2026 OneWater Marine agreed to sell a majority interest in Denison Yachting to OceanWorld Group. OneWater keeps an investment, the price was not disclosed, and closing is expected before the end of 2026. "Merged into OneWater Yacht Group September 2025" is now incomplete.
3. **The Camper & Nicholsons completion date falls before print (ch03).** The long stop is 30 September 2026, and no completion announcement was found as of 13 September. The book should print the completed position (or the extension or lapse). The 20 percent put and call from 31 December 2027 is a real 2027 event to flag forward. New fact: Sea Expandary bought 80 percent of the OceanWalker yard in Fujian (August 2026) and plans a yacht-building facility in Zhuhai. The essay's rhetorical question, "is the buyer told when C&N markets a Sea Expandary hull?", is now concrete.
4. **Ya Mon is not over (ch03).** The book says the case was "dismissed January 2025". Since then there has been appellate activity at the Eleventh Circuit (a 10 June 2026 opinion in Ya Mon Expeditions v. YATCO, No. 25-10140, on arbitration). Trade and legal press report a second amended complaint against IYBA, YBAA and brokerage firms, and the suit described as "back" (Loose Cannon, 10 August 2026). The present-tense framing is already stale in September 2026.
5. **Italian Sea Group moved well past April 2026 (ch05).**
   - 10 June 2026: Court of Florence partly revoked the protective measures.
   - 1 July 2026: TISG filed under Article 44 of the Italian Crisis Code, with reported debts of around EUR 400 million.
   - 3 July 2026: judicial commissioners appointed.
   - 9 August 2026: competitive investor process launched (asset or share deal). Binding offers are due 15 October 2026, with signing targeted for about 26 October 2026. Seven parties were reported in the data room.

   The outcome will be known before a Q1 2027 reader opens the book, so paragraph 13 needs a print-time update or a structural rewrite.
6. **EU AML Regulation from 10 July 2027 (ch03, new).** Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 applies from 10 July 2027 and brings traders in watercraft inside the EU AML perimeter as obliged entities. Legal summaries give a EUR 7.5 million threshold and a reporting duty to FIUs. It also sets a EUR 10,000 cash cap. The ch03 line "There is no financial regulator in any major jurisdiction that supervises yacht brokerage conduct" stays true for conduct, but it needs a qualifying sentence. The Regulatory coverage table needs a 2027 row. This is the single most important forward item for ch03.
7. **YORR grandfather clause closes 30 June 2027 (ch05, new).** The Superyacht Alliance register page says experienced representatives can be grandfathered only if they complete Unit 40 by 30 June 2027. The same page gives eligibility thresholds of projects over 30 metres, not 40, and three membership tiers (Principal, Experienced, Qualified). The ch05 FAQ saying "builds above 40 metres" is wrong now.
8. **Existing factual errors in ch05 FAQs.** Two FAQs say SYBAss and others "jointly founded the Yacht Owners' Register of Representatives in June 2023". That confuses the programme (YORP, announced 12 June 2023, first run end of July 2023) with the register (YORR, launched November 2024), and the register's name is wrong. Also, the lead essay says YORP launched "June 2023", while the ch05 data spread source says "July 2023". Reconcile to "announced June 2023, first run July 2023".
9. **DreAMBoat "in 18 months" is wrong (ch02, flagged for that agent).** Oceanco's own delivery release: first discussions November 2021, contract February 2022, delivery November 2025. The project finished "approximately 18 months faster than a conventional custom 110-metre build". The build itself was not 18 months. Oceanco also changed owner in August 2025 (Gabe Newell bought it from the Barwani family), which the book does not record.
10. **Yard slot claims will all read stale in 2027 and cannot be refreshed from published sources.** No current published slot guidance was found for Lürssen or Feadship. The ch02 "mid-2027", "2028 to 2029", "a 2030 Lürssen, a 2027 Heesen" and the Velema interview question "order books extending toward 2029" should be rewritten structurally, as lead time from contract, not calendar years. Project Solent (83 m Feadship, Cecil Wright acting for the owner) is confirmed for summer 2027 delivery and is outfitting at Kaag. It can stay dated, but in ch05 paragraph 63 "acted" must become "is acting".

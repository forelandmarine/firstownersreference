# Proof against fly GDN, 15 November 2026

Proof of 14 November (179 pages, 208 x 280 mm) set against the 34 photographs of *fly GDN* issue 01. Measurements are from the proof PDF; fly GDN is judged from the photographs.

## Where the proof now follows the model

- Black type on a white ground, with colour coming only from photographs.
- Three voices: serif titles, sans standfirsts, small bold sans capital crossheads.
- Chapter openers are full-bleed photographs with the title reversed out, as in fly GDN's Reykjavik and Emirates openers.
- Case and interview openers use a centred serif title, standfirst and picture band, as in Wilno, Amsterdam and Matera.
- Pictures sit at the head of a page, never part-way down a column, and no photograph is used twice.
- Every chapter ends with the same card in the same place, which does the job of "Kierunki regularne".

## Where it does not

1. **Text fills the page.** In fly GDN, text takes two of three columns and often starts a third of the way down or stops short of the foot. The proof runs two columns over the full 172 mm area, head to foot. The median text page carries 424 words, and 24 pages carry more than 800. The outer side column in specification section 3 was dropped to meet the extent target. This is the main reason the proof reads as a book and fly GDN as a magazine.
2. **Few pictures.** fly GDN has a picture on almost every page and two on most spreads. In the proof, 114 of 179 pages carry no photograph (charts are not counted as photographs), and photographs cover 24 per cent of the page area on average. There are runs of eight text and table pages at pp. 96 to 103 (chapter 06) and pp. 120 to 127 (chapter 07), and seven at pp. 156 to 162 (chapter 09).
3. **One picture shape.** fly GDN uses full-height single-column pictures, half-page pictures to the bleed, full pages facing text and pictures crossing the gutter. The proof has two shapes only: the head picture across both columns and the 60 mm band. Plates inside chapters were removed for extent, so the spreads look alike.
4. **Display scale.** fly GDN titles fill the measure at about 60 pt. The proof sets set-piece titles at about 34 pt, and the essays begin without a display title, only a drop capital.
5. **Data pages are the heaviest part of the book.** fly GDN's one data page, the violet "Południe" divider, holds a ruled table of five rows faced by a map and thumbnails. The proof has several pages of full-width tables in a row, for example the refit yards on pp. 99 to 103. The marine data opener in specification section 6 has not been built.
6. **No number-led boxes.** The "150 €, 1 balon" device in specification section 8 has not been built. It needs print-only copy, so the editors have to supply it under the content lock.
7. **Captions.** fly GDN captions most pictures in the margin beside them. Most pictures in the proof have no caption.
8. **Front matter.** The fly GDN contents is led by thumbnails with large page numbers, and the publisher's letter has a portrait. The proof's contents is a text list, and the letter is text only.
9. **Running heads use middots** ("The First Owner's Reference · 1st Edition", "CH 06 · REFIT"). This breaks the house rule, and the capitals "CH 06" read like an eyebrow. This can be fixed now.

## The trade-off

Items 1 to 3 cost pages. The specification measured about 21 per cent less copy per text page with the side column. On 147 text pages, that is about 30 more pages. The block is already 178 against a 150 to 160 target, and the content lock rules out cuts. The fly GDN density and the extent target cannot both be met without editorial cuts.

Options:

- **A. Keep the book register.** Fix item 9, add captions, and build the data opener and the number boxes when the editors supply copy. This adds about 0 to 4 pages.
- **B. Magazine treatment in the essays and cases only.** Use the side column and the tall and half-page pictures there, and keep the data and reference pages dense. This adds about 15 to 20 pages, so it needs about 35 pages of editorial cuts to reach 160.
- **C. Full fly GDN treatment.** This adds about 30 pages, to about 210, which abandons the extent target.

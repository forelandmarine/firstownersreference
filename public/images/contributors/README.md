# Contributor headshots

Drop each contributor's headshot here, then set the matching `avatar` path in
`lib/contributors.ts`. Until an image is set, the site shows a monogram
placeholder automatically.

**Only publish a photo the contributor has provided or approved for use.** The
photo is the contributor's (or a photographer's) to license, and the
publication treats its named contributors accordingly.

As of 8 August 2026, all confirmed contributors have approved the use of their
LinkedIn profile photos, so those may be used here without further sign-off.
Any new contributor added after that date needs their own approval first.

Convention:
- Filename: kebab-case of the name, e.g. `erica-lay.jpg`, `simon-roberts.jpg`.
- Square crop, at least 400x400 px, centred on the face.
- Then in `lib/contributors.ts`: `avatar: "/images/contributors/erica-lay.jpg"`.
